Privacy Policy

Last updated 17 September 2026

Receipt Ring is a small personal project. It has no advertising, no analytics and no trackers, and your data is never sold or shared for marketing.

Who runs this service

Receipt Ring is operated by [OPERATOR NAME]. Questions about this policy or about your data can be sent to [CONTACT EMAIL].

What is stored

  • Your account: your email address, an optional display name, and your password stored only as a salted scrypt hash. The password itself is never stored.
  • Receipts you save: the store, date, line items, amounts, the split between the people you named, and the receipt photo if you attach one.
  • Names you confirm: corrections you make to abbreviated item names, so the same item is recognised next time.
  • Rent entries: amounts, dates and any photo you attach, if you use the education expense tracker.
  • Your Gemini API key, if you add one: encrypted with AES-256-GCM before it is stored, and never sent back to your browser.
  • Bank transactions, for admin accounts that connect a bank: imported read-only through Plaid. The access token is encrypted at rest. Bank credentials are entered with Plaid and never reach this service.
  • A session cookie so you stay signed in, and short-lived counters used to rate limit sign-in attempts.

What is not stored

No analytics, no advertising identifiers, no third-party tracking cookies, and no location data. The only cookie is the one that keeps you signed in.

Who else sees it

  • Google: when you scan a receipt, the photo is sent to the Gemini API to be read. When you identify items, the item text is sent. Google processes this under its own terms.
  • Plaid: only if an admin account connects a bank. Plaid handles the bank login directly and returns transaction data.
  • Hosting and database providers that run the service and store its data on the operator's behalf.

Data is not sold, rented, or shared with anyone else.

How long it is kept

Your data is kept until you delete it. You can delete individual receipts at any time, and you can delete your entire account from Settings. Deleting your account removes your receipts, photos, saved item names, people, rent entries and any bank connection. Deletion is immediate and cannot be undone. Backups, if any, age out on their own schedule.

Security

Traffic is served over HTTPS. Passwords are hashed, and API keys and bank tokens are encrypted at rest. No system is perfectly secure, and this one is a personal project rather than a bank, so please do not store anything here you could not stand to lose.

Children

This service is not intended for children under 13, and accounts should not be created for them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it. Continuing to use the service after a change means the updated policy applies.
